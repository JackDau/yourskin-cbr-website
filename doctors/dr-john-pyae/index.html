About Dr John (Kyaw) Pyae

Dr John Pyae is a Fellow of the Royal Australian College of General Practitioners with a particular interest in skin cancer medicine and minor procedures. He has recently completed his Masters in Skin Cancer Medicine through the University of Queensland, and sees skin patients at YourSkinCBR Crace on Wednesdays.

John obtained his medical degree in 2012 and has almost ten years of experience in general practice, including as a well-regarded GP in Boorowa, NSW, where he gained broad experience across all aspects of general practice. His patients appreciate his warmth, respect and care — values he is passionate about bringing to every consultation.
